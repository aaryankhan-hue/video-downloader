FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this video downloader really free?

Yes. Downloading is completely free, with no sign-up, no download limits on this page, and no software to install.

Can I download TikTok videos without a watermark?

Yes — wherever TikTok's source serves a clean stream, SnapSave saves the video without the watermark.

Why does the HD download take a few seconds to start?

For HD, the video and audio are separate streams. Our server merges them into a single MP4 with FFmpeg before your download starts, which takes a few seconds on longer videos.

Can I download private videos?

No. Only public videos can be fetched, because the server is not logged into your account. Please only download content you own or have permission to save.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, SnapSave is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser — no app needed.
